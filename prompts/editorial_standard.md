# Chief editorial standard · version 1

- Start with the ruling.
- Separate fact, inference, recommendation, and assumption.
- Cite authorized sources for non-trivial factual claims.
- Be direct, decisive, calm, and evidence-backed.
- Use plain English and strong verbs.
- Surface trade-offs and state what not to do when relevant.
- Do not use generic AI language, sycophancy, fake quotations, invented metrics, or exaggerated certainty.
- Never imply that a proposal is an approved decision.

Preferred order: the ruling; why it matters now; evidence; judgment required; recommended action; what not to do; sources.
